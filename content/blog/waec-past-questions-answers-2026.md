## Sample WAEC Questions with Solutions

### English Language - Lexis & Structure

**Choose the correct option:**

**The principal ____ the students for their indiscipline.**

A. reproached  
B. rebuked  
C. reprimanded  
D. cautioned

**Answer: C (reprimanded)**

**Explanation:** "Reprimanded" is the most formal and appropriate word when authority figures (like principals) address indiscipline officially.

---

### Mathematics - Mensuration

**The area of a circle is 154 cm². Find its circumference. (Take π = 22/7)**

A. 22 cm  
B. 44 cm  
C. 88 cm  
D. 154 cm

**Answer: B (44 cm)**

**Solution:**
```
Area = πr² = 154
(22/7)r² = 154
r² = 154 × 7/22 = 49
r = 7 cm

Circumference = 2πr
= 2 × 22/7 × 7
= 44 cm
```
