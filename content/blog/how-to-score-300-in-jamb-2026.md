# How to Score 300+ in JAMB 2026 (Realistic Study Plan)

Scoring 300+ in JAMB isn't magic. It's **strategy + consistency + smart practice**. This guide breaks down the exact method used by students who consistently score 320-350 in JAMB UTME.

## What Does 300+ Really Mean?

To score 300+ out of 400, you need:
- **75 out of 100** in each subject (75% accuracy)
- Or mix it: 85 in English, 80 in Maths, 75 in Physics, 60 in Chemistry = 300 total

**Reality check:** You'll need to answer **at least 190-200 questions correctly** out of 240 total (accounting for negative marking and difficulty).

## Why Most Students Fail to Hit 300

Before the strategy, here's why 80% of students score below 250:

1. **Started too late** - 2 weeks before JAMB isn't enough
2. **No structured plan** - Random studying without focus
3. **Ignored weak topics** - Only practiced what they already knew
4. **Skipped mock exams** - Never tested themselves under real conditions
5. **Poor time management** - Spent too long on hard questions

## Time Management Strategy (Exam Day)

JAMB gives you **2.5 hours for 60 questions** = 2.5 minutes per question.

**Optimized approach:**

1. **First 30 minutes:** Answer all EASY questions first (40-45 questions)
2. **Next 60 minutes:** Tackle MEDIUM difficulty (10-12 questions)
3. **Next 45 minutes:** Attack HARD questions (5-8 questions)
4. **Last 15 minutes:** Review + guess intelligently on unsure answers

**Never spend more than 4 minutes on any single question.**

## Smart Guessing Techniques

When you're stuck, don't just pick random answers:

1. **Eliminate obviously wrong options** (usually 1-2 per question)
2. **Look for extreme values** (JAMB rarely uses them as answers)
3. **Middle values** are statistically more likely to be correct
4. **If two options are opposites**, one is usually right
5. **Trust your first instinct** (changing answers often makes it wrong)

## Mindset & Mental Preparation

Scoring 300+ is 70% strategy, 30% mindset.

**Confidence boosters:**
- Study in **25-minute focused blocks** (Pomodoro method)
- Sleep 7-8 hours (your brain consolidates learning during sleep)
- Eat protein-rich breakfast on exam day
- Arrive 1 hour early (stress kills performance)

**Avoid these:**
- All-night cramming (destroys retention)
- Comparing yourself to others
- Negative friends who say "JAMB is too hard"
- Social media during study hours
