# AeroLearn Platform: Complete Feature Overview

AeroLearn is a comprehensive, modern Student Learning Platform designed to provide an engaging, adaptive, and highly interactive educational experience. Below is a detailed breakdown of the features and modules available on the platform:

## 🧠 Core Learning & Academics
* **Learning Modules (`/learn`)**: The central hub for structured courses, lessons, and educational content.
* **Practice Center (`/practice`)**: A dedicated space for students to drill exercises and test their knowledge on specific subjects.
* **Homework Management (`/homework`)**: Seamless tracking, submission, and review of daily assignments.
* **Mock Exams (`/mock-exams`)**: Full-length, timed practice tests designed to simulate real exam environments.
* **Timetable & Scheduling (`/timetable`)**: Built-in calendar and schedule management to help students stay organized and on track.

## 🤖 AI & Adaptive Learning
* **AI Essay Grader (`/essay-grader`)**: Automated, intelligent grading of essays that provides instant feedback, grammatical corrections, and structural suggestions.
* **Weak Topics Analysis (`/weak-topics`)**: An adaptive analytics engine that identifies areas where a student is struggling and automatically suggests targeted practice to close knowledge gaps.
* **Progress Tracking (`/progress`)**: Detailed, data-rich dashboards showing learning curves, completion rates, and historical performance.

## 🏆 Gamification & Engagement
* **Leaderboards (`/leaderboard`)**: Fosters healthy competition by ranking students based on their activity, scores, and consistency.
* **Challenges (`/challenges`)**: Time-bound or objective-based tasks that encourage students to push their limits and earn rewards.
* **Virtual Store (`/store`)**: A reward system where students can spend earned points or coins on virtual goods, avatar upgrades, or unlockable content.

## 💬 Social & Communication
* **Community Forums (`/community`)**: A safe, moderated space for students to ask questions, share knowledge, and collaborate.
* **Direct Messaging (`/messages`)**: Built-in chat functionality for communicating with peers, teachers, or study groups.
* **Digital Notes (`/notes`)**: An integrated note-taking system allowing students to document key takeaways directly alongside their lessons.

## 👨‍👩‍👧 Parent & Admin Tools
* **Parent Portal (`/parent`)**: A dedicated view for parents to monitor their child's progress, upcoming homework, test scores, and overall platform engagement.
* **Admin Dashboard (`/admin`)**: A comprehensive backend for platform administrators and educators to manage content, users, and global settings.

## 💻 Cross-Platform Accessibility
* **Desktop App (`/download`)**: A fully featured Windows Desktop Application for distraction-free learning, featuring offline-capable resources and one-click installation.
* **Web Portal**: Fully responsive web application accessible from any browser or device.
