Question 1 — Algebra: linear equations
Solve 3(2x − 1) − 2(x + 4) = 9.
A. 2
B. 4
C. 5
D. 7
Correct answer: C. 5
Worked solution: Expand both brackets: 6x − 3 − 2x − 8 = 9. Collect like terms: 4x − 11 = 9, so 4x = 20 and x = 5. Check in the original equation: 3(10 − 1) − 2(5 + 4) = 27 − 18 = 9.

Question 2 — Quadratic equations
What are the roots of x² − 5x + 6 = 0?
A. −2 and −3
B. 2 and 3
C. 1 and 6
D. −1 and −6
Correct answer: B. 2 and 3
Worked solution: Find two numbers whose product is 6 and whose sum is −5: −2 and −3. Thus x² − 5x + 6 = (x − 2)(x − 3). A product is zero when at least one factor is zero, giving x = 2 or x = 3. Substitution gives 4 − 10 + 6 = 0 and 9 − 15 + 6 = 0.

Question 3 — Algebra: direct variation
y varies directly as the square of x. If y = 18 when x = 3, find y when x = 5.
A. 30
B. 36
C. 45
D. 50
Correct answer: D. 50
Worked solution: Direct variation with the square means y = kx². Substitute the known pair: 18 = k × 3² = 9k, so k = 2. At x = 5, y = 2 × 25 = 50. Equivalently, y/18 = 5²/3² = 25/9, giving y = 50.

Question 4 — Plane geometry: area of a trapezium
A trapezium has parallel sides of lengths 8 cm and 14 cm. The perpendicular distance between these sides is 5 cm. What is its area?
A. 55 cm²
B. 70 cm²
C. 110 cm²
D. 40 cm²
Correct answer: A. 55 cm²
Worked solution: Area of a trapezium = ½ × (sum of the parallel sides) × perpendicular height. Therefore A = ½ × (8 + 14) × 5 = ½ × 22 × 5 = 55 cm². The height is the perpendicular separation, not a sloping side. Two identical copies make a parallelogram of base 22 cm and height 5 cm, so one has half of 110 cm².

Question 5 — Trigonometry: right-angled triangles
A straight ladder 10 m long rests against a vertical wall on horizontal ground. It makes an angle of 30° with the ground. How high up the wall does it reach?
A. 10√3 m
B. 5√3 m
C. 5 m
D. 10 m
Correct answer: C. 5 m
Worked solution: The ladder is the hypotenuse of a right-angled triangle. The vertical height h is opposite the 30° angle, so sin 30° = h/10. Since sin 30° = ½, h = 10 × ½ = 5 m. The horizontal distance would be 10 cos 30° = 5√3 m; it is not the requested height.

Question 6 — Probability: sampling without replacement
A bag contains 3 red balls and 2 blue balls of the same size. Two balls are drawn at random, one after the other, without replacement. What is the probability that both are red?
A. 9/25
B. 3/10
C. 2/5
D. 1/5
Correct answer: B. 3/10
Worked solution: The probability that the first ball is red is 3/5. If it is red, 2 red balls remain out of 4 balls, so the conditional probability that the second is red is 2/4. Multiply: (3/5) × (2/4) = 6/20 = 3/10. Checking by combinations: there are 3 red pairs out of 10 possible unordered pairs, also giving 3/10.

Question 7 — Statistics: arithmetic mean
The scores of five students in a short test are 4, 7, 7, 10 and 12. What is the arithmetic mean score?
A. 7
B. 7.5
C. 10
D. 8
Correct answer: D. 8
Worked solution: Add all five scores: 4 + 7 + 7 + 10 + 12 = 40. Divide by the number of scores, 5: mean = 40/5 = 8. The middle score, 7, is the median, not the mean. As a check, deviations from 8 are −4, −1, −1, 2 and 4, which sum to zero.

Question 8 — Coordinate geometry: distance between two points
Find the distance between P(1, 2) and Q(7, 10) on a Cartesian plane.
A. 10 units
B. 14 units
C. 100 units
D. 2√7 units
Correct answer: A. 10 units
Worked solution: The horizontal difference is 7 − 1 = 6 and the vertical difference is 10 − 2 = 8. By Pythagoras, PQ = √(6² + 8²) = √(36 + 64) = √100 = 10 units. This is a 6–8–10 right triangle. Adding 6 and 8 gives a two-segment path of 14 units, not the straight-line distance.
